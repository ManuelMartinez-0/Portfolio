About Me

Computer Science Student, Systems Developer, and Problem Solver

Who I Am

I am a junior undergraduate student pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at the University of Central Florida in Orlando. My studies focus on low-level systems programming, algorithms, computer architecture, and computing security. I take a hands-on, empirical approach to software development—prioritizing clean memory management, thorough terminal debugging, and structured documentation.

Goals & Career Trajectory

Academically, I am advancing through core systems coursework while contributing to undergraduate research that investigates technical onboarding hurdles for computer science students, comparing the usability of Linux and Windows toolchains.

Professionally, I am actively preparing for software engineering roles in mission-critical industries such as defense and aerospace engineering (including the Lockheed Martin CWEP program). My objective is to contribute to systems-level C/C++ applications that demand high reliability, dynamic memory verification, and disciplined testing.

Interests & Collaboration

Outside of academics, I follow emerging developments in computer architecture, explore cybersecurity topics, and stay active in the UCF computing community.
