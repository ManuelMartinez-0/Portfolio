About Me

Computer Science Student, Systems Developer, and Problem Solver

Who I Am

I am a Junior working towards a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science here at the University of Central Florida in Orlando. My coursework so far includes studies of low-level systems programming, algorithms, computer architecture, and computing security. I prefer to take an empirical approach to building software, striving to develop robust programs that use memory correctly, include thorough debugging in the terminal, and are well-documented.

Goals & Career Trajectory

I am currently taking a variety of courses as part of my undergraduate studies in computer science as well as contributing to research that aims to solve problems that computer science students face during their onboarding process to using computer systems. This research specifically aims to compare and contrast the use of Linux and Windows toolchains.

As I go through university and explore potential software engineering roles in various industries such as defense and aerospace engineering (including Lockheed Martin CWEP program) I look to add value to systems-level C/C++ applications that stress high reliability, the need for verification of dynamic memory allocation, and rigorous testing.

Interests & Collaboration

In my free time, I am a peer reviewer and tech writer. I especially enjoy trying to break down how to setup and get going with particularly complicated pieces of software for others to read and reproduce. Right now, this means keeping an eye on some of the recent trends in computer architecture, reading up on computer security from time to time, and trying to get more involved in the rest of the UCF computing community.
